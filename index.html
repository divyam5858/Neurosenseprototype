About NeuroSense

NeuroSense combines advanced AI technology with clinical expertise to provide comprehensive support for neurodegenerative disease management. Our platform specializes in:

  • Alzheimer's Disease (AD) - Progressive memory and cognitive decline
  • Parkinson's Disease (PD) - Movement disorder affecting motor function
  • Dementia with Lewy Bodies (DLB) - Cognitive fluctuations and visual hallucinations
  • Frontotemporal Dementia (FTD) - Personality and language changes
